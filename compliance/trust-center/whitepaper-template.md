# Whitepaper Template

**Document Type**: Whitepaper  
**Version**: 1.0  
**Template Date**: 2026-06-15

## Purpose

This template provides a standard structure for PacketPath whitepapers published on the trust center.

## Whitepaper Structure

### Title Page

- **Title**: Clear, descriptive title
- **Subtitle**: Brief description of content
- **Author**: Author name and title
- **Date**: Publication date
- **Version**: Document version
- **Abstract**: 150-200 word summary

### Table of Contents

- Section headings with page numbers
- List of figures and tables
- List of abbreviations

### Executive Summary

- Overview of the whitepaper
- Key findings or recommendations
- Target audience
- Business value

### Introduction

- Background and context
- Problem statement
- Objectives
- Scope

### Main Content

#### Section 1: [Topic Overview]

- Definition and explanation
- Industry context
- Relevance to PacketPath

#### Section 2: [Technical Details]

- Technical approach
- Architecture diagrams
- Implementation details
- Code examples (if applicable)

#### Section 3: [Benefits and Value]

- Business benefits
- Security benefits
- Compliance benefits
- ROI analysis

#### Section 4: [Case Studies]

- Real-world examples
- Success stories
- Lessons learned

#### Section 5: [Best Practices]

- Recommendations
- Implementation guidance
- Common pitfalls

#### Section 6: [Conclusion]

- Summary of key points
- Call to action
- Future outlook

### Appendices

- Appendix A: Technical specifications
- Appendix B: Compliance mappings
- Appendix C: References
- Appendix D: Glossary

## Formatting Guidelines

### Typography

- **Font**: Arial or Helvetica
- **Body text**: 11pt
- **Headings**: 14-18pt
- **Line spacing**: 1.5
- **Margins**: 1 inch

### Visual Elements

- **Diagrams**: High-resolution, labeled
- **Tables**: Clean formatting, headers
- **Charts**: Clear labels, legends
- **Screenshots**: Annotated, relevant

### Citations

- **Style**: APA or IEEE
- **Format**: In-text citations with reference list
- **Links**: Hyperlinked where appropriate

## Content Guidelines

### Tone

- Professional and authoritative
- Clear and concise
- Technical but accessible
- Vendor-neutral where possible

### Length

- **Executive summary**: 1-2 pages
- **Main content**: 10-20 pages
- **Total length**: 15-25 pages

### Audience

- Target audience clearly identified
- Technical level appropriate for audience
- Industry jargon explained
- Acronyms defined
