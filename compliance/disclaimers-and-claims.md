# Disclaimers and Claims

## Important Disclaimers

### No Certification or Authorization

PacketPath is **NOT**:
- FedRAMP Authorized
- FedRAMP Certified
- CMMC Certified
- SOC 2 Certified
- HIPAA Certified
- ISO 27001 Certified
- GDPR Certified
- DoD Approved
- Government Approved

### Compliance Readiness Only

The materials in this compliance directory represent:
- **Readiness**: PacketPath is built toward compliance frameworks
- **Evidence**: Controls and documentation exist to support review
- **Alignment**: Security practices align with framework requirements
- **Preparation**: Materials support future formal assessments

These materials do **NOT** represent:
- Independent certification
- Third-party audit results
- Government authorization
- Legal compliance guarantees
- Regulatory approval

### Production PHI/CUI Use

For production use with Protected Health Information (PHI) or Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI):
- An approved hosting boundary is required
- Signed Business Associate Agreements (BAA) are required
- Vendor agreements must be in place
- Formal risk assessment is required
- Incident response procedures must be established

The current deployment on Render/Neon is suitable for **demo and development only**.

### Legal Disclaimer

This compliance package is for internal security review and future assessment preparation. It does not constitute legal advice. Occu-Med should consult with legal counsel for compliance requirements specific to their use case.

## Allowed Claims

If supported by evidence, PacketPath may truthfully claim:

### FedRAMP/NIST/HIPAA
- "Built toward FedRAMP Moderate readiness."
- "Maintains a FedRAMP-style evidence package."
- "Includes NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 control mapping."
- "Includes NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 3 / CUI readiness mapping."
- "Includes HIPAA Security Rule safeguard mapping."

### Security Features
- "Implements tamper-evident signing audit trails."
- "Supports immutable-style audit evidence and document hash verification."
- "Uses automated security scanning and SBOM generation."
- "Implements AES-256-GCM encryption for sensitive data."
- "Maintains comprehensive audit logging."

### Multi-Framework
- "Built toward FedRAMP Moderate readiness and maintains a multi-framework compliance-readiness package covering NIST SP 800-53, NIST SP 800-171, HIPAA Security Rule safeguards, SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria readiness, GDPR privacy readiness, OWASP ASVS application security, and ISO 27001-style ISMS controls. Formal certification/authorization has not yet been completed."

## Forbidden Claims

Do **NOT** claim any of the following under any circumstances:

- "FedRAMP Authorized"
- "FedRAMP Certified"
- "CMMC Certified"
- "SOC 2 Certified"
- "SOC 2 Type II Certified"
- "HIPAA Certified"
- "HIPAA Compliant" (without qualification)
- "ISO 27001 Certified"
- "ISO 27001 Compliant" (without qualification)
- "GDPR Certified"
- "GDPR Compliant" (without qualification)
- "DoD Approved"
- "Government Approved"
- "Meets all regulatory requirements"

## Evidence-Based Claims

All claims must be:
1. **Supported by evidence**: Code, tests, documentation, or artifacts
2. **Truthful**: No exaggeration or misrepresentation
3. **Qualified**: Use "readiness," "aligned," "built toward" instead of "certified"
4. **Contextual**: Explain what is and isn't covered

## Framework-Specific Qualifiers

### FedRAMP
- "FedRAMP-targeted" or "FedRAMP-ready" (not "FedRAMP Authorized")
- "FedRAMP Moderate readiness" (not "FedRAMP Moderate compliant")

### SOC 2
- "SOC 2 readiness" or "SOC 2 Type I readiness" (not "SOC 2 Certified")
- "Trust Services Criteria aligned" (not "TSC compliant")

### HIPAA
- "HIPAA Security Rule aligned" or "HIPAA safeguards implemented" (not "HIPAA Compliant")
- "PHI-ready architecture" (not "PHI compliant")

### GDPR
- "GDPR readiness" or "GDPR privacy controls" (not "GDPR Compliant")
- "Privacy by design implemented" (not "GDPR certified")

### ISO 27001
- "ISO 27001-style ISMS" or "ISO 27001-aligned" (not "ISO 27001 Certified")
- "ISMS readiness package" (not "ISMS compliant")

## Customer Communication

When discussing compliance with customers or vendors:

1. **Be transparent**: Explain what is implemented and what requires formal assessment
2. **Provide evidence**: Share relevant documentation and artifacts
3. **Set expectations**: Clarify that certification requires third-party audit
4. **Maintain integrity**: Do not overstate capabilities

### Example Statement

"PacketPath is built toward FedRAMP Moderate readiness and maintains a multi-framework compliance-readiness package. We have implemented controls aligned with NIST SP 800-53, NIST SP 800-171, HIPAA Security Rule safeguards, SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria, GDPR privacy requirements, OWASP ASVS, and ISO 27001-style ISMS. Formal certification/authorization requires third-party assessment and is not yet completed."
