## How We Use Your Data

### Primary Purposes

1. **Electronic Signature Processing**: To facilitate document signing and verification
2. **User Account Management**: To provide authentication and access control
3. **Case Management**: To manage occupational health cases and associated documents
4. **Security Monitoring**: To detect and prevent fraud and unauthorized access
5. **Compliance**: To comply with legal and regulatory requirements

### Legal Basis for Processing

- **Contract Performance**: When you sign documents or use our services (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR)
- **Legal Obligation**: When required by law (e.g., HIPAA) (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR)
- **Legitimate Interest**: For security monitoring and fraud prevention (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR)

## Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

- **Encryption**: AES-256-GCM encryption for sensitive data at rest
- **Transmission**: TLS 1.3 for data in transit
- **Access Control**: Role-based access control and multi-factor authentication
- **Audit Logging**: Comprehensive logging of all data access
- **Regular Testing**: Security scanning and vulnerability assessments
